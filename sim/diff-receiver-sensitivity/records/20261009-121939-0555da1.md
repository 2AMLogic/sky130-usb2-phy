## Method

- Stimulus: DP = VCM + VDIFF/2, DM = VCM - VDIFF/2, VCM in {0.8, 1.65, 2.5} V (one receiver instance per VCM, suffix cm0p80 / cm1p65 / cm2p50 in measurement names); VDIFF swept -400 mV to +400 mV, 1 mV step, both polarities; supply = swept I/O rail vsup.
- Output decision threshold: RXD crosses VDD/2 (node `half`); RXD=1 means DP more positive than DM (J).
- Input-referred threshold `vth_*`: the first VDIFF (rising sweep from -400 mV) at which RXD = VDD/2, from `.meas dc ... WHEN v(rxd)=v(half) CROSS=1`; checked to lie strictly inside (-200 mV, +200 mV). A non-finite or absent crossing fails the corner.
- Strict >200 mV wording preserved: correct polarity is checked over the whole range beyond the boundary, `rxd_pos_min_*` = minimum of RXD over VDIFF in [+200.5, +400] mV and `rxd_neg_max_*` = maximum of RXD over VDIFF in [-400, -200.5] mV (an inverted or non-monotonic receiver fails). `rxd_p200_*` / `rxd_m200_*` are the +/-200 mV boundary probes, checked with the same bounds.
- Output bounds (engineering criteria for the 3.3 V-domain RXD, not USB driver VOH/VOL limits): high >= 0.9 x VDD and low <= 0.1 x VDD of the corner supply.
- `isup_ua_*`: supply current of that receiver at VDIFF = 0 (bias behaviour); sanity bound 1-500 uA, not a spec row.
- Sweep resolution: 1 mV, so `vth_*` is resolved to the interpolated crossing between 1 mV points; the 0.5 mV guard (200.5 mV) keeps the +/-200 mV points out of the beyond-boundary windows.
