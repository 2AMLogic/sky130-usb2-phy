## Method

- Stimulus and measurements as sim/diff-receiver-sensitivity (VDIFF = DP - DM swept -400 ... +400 mV at 1 mV, VCM in {0.8, 1.65, 2.5} V, one receiver instance per VCM), with the supply, temperature and library section of the corner and the extra `skew_v` input-pair offset parameter (0 in the nominal campaign).
- Row measurements per VCM: `vth_*` = first VDIFF at which RXD crosses VDD/2, must lie strictly inside (-200 mV, +200 mV); `rxd_p200_*` / `rxd_m200_*` = RXD at VDIFF = +/-200 mV, must be >= 0.9 x VDD / <= 0.1 x VDD of the corner supply (a valid logic level at the row limit). A sample with no crossing in +/-400 mV has no `vth` and is reported by klt yield as `errored`; the lint requires errored = 0 for a PASS record.
- Each corner is its own population (one `klt sim` Monte Carlo request, backend batch, per corner); nothing is pooled across corners. `klt yield-samples` pairs each nominal campaign with its negative-control campaign (same deck, same seeds, the deterministic skew below), and `klt yield` grades the pair against the row limits with target_yield 0.99 at 95 % confidence.
- Negative control (deterministic): the control campaign applies a fixed 0.25 V differential input offset (`skew_v`), which moves every sample's differential threshold to about -0.28 ... -0.33 V, past the 200 mV row. The yield report must flag it `detected` for every measurement; a campaign whose control is `not_detected` cannot be a PASS record. `rxd_p200_*` is not degraded by the declared skew by construction (a +0.25 V offset only raises the output at VDIFF = +200 mV), so its control is expected to read `not_detected` and is not graded; `klt yield` therefore also prints a run-level warning that at least one negative control did not show the expected degradation, which is this expected case, not a failure.
- Mismatch activity: `klt sim --dry-run` reports mosfet and resistor mismatch as active for `*_mm` sections; the per-sample spread of `vth_*` in the report (stddev in each yield report's `distribution`) is the evidence that mismatch reached the g5v0d10v5 devices used here (a stddev of zero would fail the record).
- PDK: the pinned open_pdks revision of sim/pdk-pins.json (current pin); each record states the revision and the model library sha256.
- Corner `tt`: typical corner with mismatch enabled.
- Corner `binding`: binding corner of the 45-point record sim/diff-receiver-sensitivity/records/20261009-121939-0555da1.md: the largest |vth| (-75.97 mV, vth_cm2p50_v) is at sf / 100 C / 3.00 V.
