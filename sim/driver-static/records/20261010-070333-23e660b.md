## Method

- Supply = swept I/O rail vsup (3.0 / 3.3 / 3.6 V). Inputs are VDD-domain logic: logic 1 wired to the supply node, logic 0 to ground. One DC sweep of the forcing source vf (0-3.65 V, 50 mV step; the sweep runs one step past 3.6 V so that the 3.6 V point is interior; max/min are taken over 0-3.6 V only) is the only analysis; every quantity other than the leakage sweep is sweep-independent and read at the first point (AT=0). Each test uses its own copy of the driver.
- VOH/VOL load conditions, from the USB 2.0 specification section 7.1.1.1 (full-speed driver characteristics): the static output must be below VOL(max) 0.3 V with a 1.5 kohm load to 3.6 V and above VOH(min) 2.8 V with a 15 kohm load to ground. `voh_*_v`: driven high (TXDx=1, DRVEN=OE=1) into 15 kohm to ground; `vol_*_v`: driven low into 1.5 kohm to 3.6 V. The ratified row's parenthetical '(1.5 kohm pull-up to 3.6 V)' sits on the VOH/VOL cell as a whole; the literal reading for VOH is measured too (`voh15_*_v`: driven high into 1.5 kohm to 3.6 V) and checked against the same 2.8-3.6 V. The reading of the parenthetical is raised in spec/decision-records/0004-fs-driver-voh-vol-load-conditions.md (proposed); no ratified value is changed.
- Driver output resistance (total, enabled, static): the pad is forced by an ideal source 0.3 V away from the rail the driver is pulling to (VDD - 0.3 V when driving high, 0.3 V when driving low); R = 0.3 V / |I(pad)|. This is a large-signal resistance at the static drive point, includes the output FET on-resistance (P for high, N for low) and the series poly resistor RSER, and is checked for each line and each polarity against 28-44 ohm.
- Enable truth table: all four TXDP/TXDM data combinations (including SE0 = 00 and 11) x all four DRVEN/OE combinations, each pad loaded by 10 kohm to VDD/2, so a driven 1, a driven 0 and a released pad are distinct. Functional bounds (fractions of the corner supply, engineering criteria, not USB limits): driven 1 >= 0.95, driven 0 <= 0.05, released (DRVEN=0 or OE=0) within 0.48-0.52. Names `tt_<line>_d<TXDP><TXDM>_e<DRVEN><OE>_v`.
- Disabled-pad leakage (engineering data, no ratified limit, none applied): instance lka disabled by OE=0 (DRVEN=1, TXDP=1), D+ forced by vf from 0 to 3.6 V while D- is held at VDD/2; instance lkb disabled by DRVEN=0 (OE=1, TXDM=1), D- forced by vf while D+ is held at VDD/2. Current positive out of the pad into the forcing source; reported at 0 / 1.5 / 3.0 / 3.6 V and its max/min over the sweep; `leak_*_mid_na` is the held line's current. Forcing above the corner supply (up to 3.6 V at a 3.0 V rail) forward-biases the output PMOS drain junction; that current is part of the data.
- `isup_static_ua`: supply current of one extra driver instance enabled and driving J (TXDP=1, TXDM=0) into open pads: bias plus leakage; data only.
- A missing or non-finite value of any measurement fails the corner (harness rule).
- Resistor/capacitor coverage: each point's process section is one of 25 pinned-library sections = 5 MOS corners x {typical, ll, hh, hl, lh}; the section -> (MOS file, resistor set, capacitor set) mapping and the resolved library includes are recorded in evidence.json (`sections`) and checked by the lint against the approved map (sim/corners-resistor-sets.json, spec/decision-records/0005-resistor-sheet-resistance-corner-coverage.md). The bare sections `ll`, `hh`, `hl`, `lh` carry the tt MOS corner.
- Compatibility: every bound and check above is unchanged and applies at all 225 points; this record is a supplement to, not a replacement for, the 45-point records (res_typical only), which are unchanged and keep their own frozen matrix.
