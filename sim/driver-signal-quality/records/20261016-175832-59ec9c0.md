## Method

- Test load (carried from the source testbench): 50 pF from each of D+ and D- to ground, no resistor; the ratified rise/fall row names only a capacitance. Supply = swept I/O rail vsup (3.0 / 3.3 / 3.6 V).
- Inputs: VDD-domain logic built from B sources scaled by v(vdd) (logic 1 = the corner supply). DRVEN = OE = 1 for instance xa; TXDP = ctl, TXDM = not ctl; ctl rises at 100 ns and falls at 200 ns, 0.5 ns input edges. Transition 1 (100 ns): D+ rises, D- falls; transition 2 (200 ns): D+ falls, D- rises. Window 0-300 ns, max step 20 ps.
- Rise/fall time: 10 %-90 % of the corner supply on each line separately (`trise_dp_ns`, `tfall_dp_ns`, `trise_dm_ns`, `tfall_dm_ns`; `.meas TRIG v(dXn) VAL=0.1 ... TARG v(dXn) VAL=0.9`, dXn = v(dX)/v(vdd)), each checked against 4-20 ns.
- Rise/fall matching: ratio of rise time to fall time, checked against [0.9, 1.1] for four pairings so that no single pairing hides a failing half: per line (`trf_dp_ratio` = trise_dp/tfall_dp, `trf_dm_ratio` = trise_dm/tfall_dm) and per transition across the pair (`trf_x1_ratio` = trise_dp/tfall_dm at transition 1, `trf_x2_ratio` = trise_dm/tfall_dp at transition 2). 'Within 10 %' is read as 0.9 <= tr/tf <= 1.1 (inclusive, symmetric in the ratio).
- Crossover voltage: v(dp) at the instant v(dp) = v(dm), at the rising (`vcross_rise_v`, transition 1) and the falling (`vcross_fall_v`, transition 2) crossing, each checked against 1.3-2.0 V.
- Single crossing: the first and second D+/D- crossings over the whole window (`tx_first_ns`, `tx_second_ns`, CROSS=1/2) must coincide within 1 ps with the rising and falling crossings, and the last crossing (`tx_last_ns`, CROSS=LAST) with the second one, so any extra crossing (ringing, a non-monotonic edge) fails the corner.
- Monotonic edges: dV/dt of each line (B source ddt, V/ns) masked to the 10 %-90 % band of its own edge; outside the band the mask is +1 for a rising and -1 for a falling window. `slope_rise_*_min_vns` (MIN over the rising window) must be > 0 and `slope_fall_*_max_vns` (MAX over the falling window) < 0, for both lines and both directions. Windows: 100-200 ns for transition 1, 200-300 ns for transition 2.
- Engineering data, no ratified limit and no check: overshoot above VDD and undershoot below VSS over the whole window (`over_*_mv`, `under_*_mv`; source of any pre-edge dip is gate-to-output Miller-cap coupling, see design/README.md), data-to-pad delay at 50 % (`tpd_*_ns`).
- Enable/disable transitions (instances xb: TXDP=1, TXDM=0; xc: TXDP=0, TXDM=1; DRVEN = 1, OE rises at 100 ns and falls at 200 ns): each pad forced to VDD/2 by an ideal source; current positive out of the driver. Reported per line: disabled current before enable (`*_off_pre_na`, max |I|, 20-99 ns); the time |I| first rises through 10 mA (above the capacitive coupling current, below the drive current) (`*_enable_at_ns`, absolute; OE rises at 100.0-100.5 ns); enabled current (`*_on_ma`, mean over 150-199 ns; only its sign is checked: a pad driven high must source, one driven low must sink); the time |I| last falls through 1 uA (`*_release_at_ns`, absolute; OE falls at 200.0-200.5 ns); disabled current after release (`*_off_post_na`, max |I|, 250-300 ns); and the peak wrong-polarity current over 99-300 ns (`*_wrongpol_pk_ma`: sinking on a line commanded high or sourcing on one commanded low, i.e. a glitch toward the wrong rail). These are engineering data; no leakage or glitch limit exists and none is applied.
- A missing or non-finite value of any measurement fails the corner (harness rule).
- Resistor/capacitor coverage: each point's process section is one of 25 pinned-library sections = 5 MOS corners x {typical, ll, hh, hl, lh}; the section -> (MOS file, resistor set, capacitor set) mapping and the resolved library includes are recorded in evidence.json (`sections`) and checked by the lint against the approved map (sim/corners-resistor-sets.json, spec/decision-records/0005-resistor-sheet-resistance-corner-coverage.md). The bare sections `ll`, `hh`, `hl`, `lh` carry the tt MOS corner.
- Compatibility: every bound and check above is unchanged and applies at all 225 points; this record is a supplement to, not a replacement for, the 45-point records (res_typical only), which are unchanged and keep their own frozen matrix.
