## Method

- Stimulus and measurements as sim/se-receiver-dp-thresholds and sim/se-receiver-dm-thresholds: the line input swept 0 - 3.0 V at 1 mV; supply, temperature and library section of the corner.
- Row measurements: `vth_v` = first rising crossing of RXD through VDD/2, must lie strictly inside (0.8 V, 2.0 V); `rxd_low_max_v` = maximum of RXD over input 0 - 0.8 V, must be <= 0.1 x VDD; `rxd_high_min_v` = minimum of RXD over input 2.0 - 3.0 V, must be >= 0.9 x VDD. A sample with no rising crossing (an inverted or dead receiver) has no `vth_v` and is reported by klt yield as `errored`; the lint requires errored = 0 for a PASS record.
- Each (receiver, corner) is its own population: one `klt sim` Monte Carlo request, backend batch, per receiver per corner; nothing is pooled across corners or across D+/D-. `klt yield-samples` pairs each nominal campaign with its negative-control campaign (same deck, same seeds, the deterministic skew below), and `klt yield` grades the pair against the row limits with target_yield 0.99 at 95 % confidence.
- Negative control (deterministic): the control campaign lengthens R2 from 14 to 60 (fixed divider-ratio skew), which moves VREF and so the threshold above 2.0 V at every sample. The yield report must flag it `detected` for every measurement that the skew degrades (at least `vth_v`); a campaign whose control is `not_detected` for `vth_v` cannot be a PASS record. `rxd_low_max_v` is not degraded by the declared skew by construction (a higher VREF only keeps the output low over 0 - 0.8 V), so its control is expected to read `not_detected` and is not graded; `klt yield` therefore also prints a run-level warning that at least one negative control did not show the expected degradation, which is this expected case, not a failure.
- Mismatch activity: as in sim/diff-receiver-mismatch; the stddev of `vth_v` in each yield report's `distribution` is the evidence that mismatch reached the divider and the comparator (a stddev of zero would fail the record).
- PDK: the pinned open_pdks revision of sim/pdk-pins.json (current pin); each record states the revision and the model library sha256.
- Disclosure: the D+ and D- netlists are identical apart from the port names and both campaigns use base seed 166, so their per-sample mismatch draws, and therefore their sample sets and yield numbers, are numerically identical (dp and dm rows of the record agree to the last digit). The two receivers are separate cells and each row is a valid sample of its own circuit, but the D+ and D- results are one population counted for two receivers, not two independent draws.
- Corner `tt`: typical corner with mismatch enabled.
- Corner `binding`: binding corner of the 45-point records sim/se-receiver-dp-thresholds/records/20261009-142213-484240f.md and sim/se-receiver-dm-thresholds/records/20261009-142853-484240f.md: the lowest threshold (1.2505 V, 450 mV above VIL) is at ff / 100 C / 3.00 V; the highest (1.5136 V at ss / -40 C / 3.60 V) is 486 mV below VIH.
