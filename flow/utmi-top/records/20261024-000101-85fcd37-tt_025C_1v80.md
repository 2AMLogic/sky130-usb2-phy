### Structural finding

### Clocks

The design has TWO clocks -- `clk_144` at 144 MHz and `clk_utmi` at 30 MHz, asynchronous to each other and joined by the CDC in rtl/usb_rx_cdc.v and the 2-flop synchronisers in rtl/usb_utmi_top.v. `klt synthesize`'s request carries a single `constraints.clock_period_ns`; the committed request sets 33.333 (the 30 MHz period) and that value only steers ABC's delay target. It does not describe the 144 MHz domain, and no per-clock constraint was applied. Nothing in this record is a timing claim for either domain.
